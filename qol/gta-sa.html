Live lab install: San Andreas 1.0 with Silent's ASI loader, ModLoader 0.3.10, and CLEO 4.4.4. Packs live as folders under modloader\.

The feel (read this first!!!)
  • DualSense + PlayStation button glyphs, ControlsSet=2 (IV/V-style). Vibration on. Free-aim off.
  • 60 FPS windowed 1920×1080 (Alt+Enter). Auto-pause off. Pool/minigames drop to 30.
  • F2 save anywhere (on foot, not in a vehicle).
  • Tab / Q weapon wheel (slow-mo on).
  • Caps Lock or L3+R3 Focus: on-foot Area Kill + car slow-mo. No blue wash. Meter stays full.
  • Pad driveby: L1 aim, R1 shoot; D-pad left/right cycles weapons.
  • Real Traffic Fix — smarter traffic AI: realistic speeds, lane discipline, fewer suicidal cut-ins. Defaults are fine. No other traffic-AI mod.
  • VehDeform — real-time dents on every vehicle. Cheats: VDCOL toggle, VDRES reset, VDBG debug. Not with LocalDent.
  • Overdose Effects — huge explosions, thicker smoke and fire, new bullet impacts, sparks, and muzzle flashes. Own Mod Loader folder. Don't pair it with other effects packs.

Traffic / Physics

Mod What it does here
Real Traffic Fix 2.2.1 beta Junior_Djjr (MixMods). NPC cars keep realistic speeds, slow for curves and obstacles, and stop cutting in front of you. Trucks and buses stay in the slow lane. Fixes cars stuck in the ground. Silent's ASI Loader + Mod Loader. SA 1.0 US. modloader\RealTrafficFix\RealTrafficFix.SA.asi and RealTrafficFix.ini. Defaults are fine. The INI sets cruise speed, lane behavior, horn frequency, and speed bumps. TestMode = 1 shows debug info. source. Don't run it with any other traffic-AI mod.
VehDeform 1.3c ZZPuma. Crashes, bullets, melee, and explosions dent the mesh on cars, bikes, boats, and aircraft. Heavy hits can bend hoods, pop tires and parts, and kill the engine. Silent's ASI Loader, SilentPatch, Open Limit Adjuster, and Mod Loader. modloader\VehDeform\VehDeform.SA.asi and VehDeform_CFG.ini. Free public Patreon post; archive password is 123 (stated on the post). Cheats: VDCOL toggles all deformation (missions it can break), VDRES resets the current vehicle mesh, VDBG debug (arrow keys). INI: impact strength, whether peds / player / engine can be killed, tire and part pop thresholds, allowed vehicle types, ignore list (Rhino, Skimmer, RC Baron by default). Turn off DeformCollision if you use SA-MP. Conflicts with LocalDent — install only one.

Visual

Mod What it does here
SkyGfx SA 4.2b Active profile is PS2-like (building/vehicle pipe, color filter, radiosity). F10 cycles INIs, F11 reloads.
Project 2DFX LOD/corona lights. Pack's old limit adjuster skipped — we use Open Limit Adjuster.
Overdose Effects 1.5 Function-X- (thanks DK22Pac). Final official release, Dec 31 2012. Bigger, more intense explosions, fire, smoke, bullet impacts, sparks, blood, muzzle flashes, and water splashes. Also butterflies, fireflies, falling leaves and petals, fireworks, sewer steam, distinct grenade / rocket / satchel / petrol-pump explosions, lamp-post sparks, and burning barrels. Punchy, a bit over the top. Nothing beyond the game. SA 1.0 US. Mod Loader so originals are not overwritten. modloader\OverdoseEffects\models\effects.fxp, models\effectsPC.txd, data\object.dat, data\surfinfo.dat. Without Mod Loader, copy the archive's Models and Data folders over the game folder after backing up those four files. Get "Overdose Effects V1.5 Part One" from the author's upload. There is no Part Two. Anything labeled v1.6 is an unofficial re-upload. official topic. No config file. effects.fxp and effectsPC.txd are the look. object.dat links objects to the new effects and makes lamp posts and traffic lights take more collision damage; surfinfo.dat changes bullet impacts on wood and grass. Leave the two .dat files out to keep just the visuals. The ~16 MB effectsPC.txd can cost FPS in big explosions. MixSets FxEmissionRateMult can cut particle density. Works with SkyGfx and Project2DFX. Don't combine with other packs that replace effects.fxp / effectsPC.txd (IMFX effects, NextGen/Remaster Effects, Overdose retexture packs). Other mods that ship their own object.dat or surfinfo.dat can clash.
